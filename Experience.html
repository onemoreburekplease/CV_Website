Graphic designer, Web developer

(2024 - current)

My main responsibilities include creating marketing materials, such as movie promotions, thumbnails, website animations AND also designing and coding the visual identity of websites (T2ToGo, T+ and more). At FORA d.o.o. I had and still have tremendous opportunities to grow and learn, both coding and marketing. I use special tools to receive feedback on the effectiveness of my designs and implementations, such as clicks that lead to views, most common times of streaming etc.
