FORA d.o.o.

Graphic Design and App development

(2024 - current)

FORA is an IT company that develops streaming platforms. Some of its customers include Donald Trump (Truth+), T2 (T2ToGo), UAE (Rotana) and many more.

I create Ads for movies, shows, new channel launches and sporting events. I'm responsible for the brainstorming, I sketch it roughly out, and finally after acquiring feedback, finish it in either Photoshop, Illustrator, Blender or any combination thereof.

At FORA I also currently do and am studying Web development and UI/UX design. I create logos, icons, UI setups and more. I also do and am slowly studying programming in general.

Ministry of Digitalization RS

Event Organizer and Designer

(2022 - 2025)

Campaigns had to have a specific identity and feel. Design was sometimes print based, leading me to create broschures, posters, huge stickers and even whole billboards.

I've also worked on digital design, creating social media posts, animations, collaborations with other RS institutions and much more.
