About Me
Graphic & Motion Designer

Zivijo!

My name is Amar Zulić, I'm a Graphic designer, Motion designer and Web Designer from Ljubljana, Slovenia.

I'm currently a 26 year old student finishing my studies.

I have completed my Bachelor's degree in Marketing at the University of Ljubljana, School of Economics, and am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Tourism Marketing.

01 — Technical Info

Design, motion, and code.

I have been drawing since childhood. I tend to practice every day, even now. Being a strong hobby of mine, drawing sparked my interest in other forms of visual creativity, so I started teaching myself how to create digitally. I started experimenting with Photoshop about 10 years ago, which I used to generally create custom gifts for friends and family members. Later on, I started learning other software, eventually moving to animation, 3D design, and motion design. My last couple of jobs also taught me web design, so I've coded this entire website myself, and I really enjoyed the process.

I am extremely interested in everything. I love 3D design as much as I like, for example, coding in Android Studio. Some fields of design I realistically know more, such as motion design in After Effects or vector design in Illustrator, but I am always looking for opportunities to learn and explore elsewhere.
